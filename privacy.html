Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

Welcome to FactLoop ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information of our viewers, visitors, and users of our digital content and services.

1. Information We Collect

FactLoop operates public content channels on YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We only collect standard operational data:

  • Public Social Interactions: Public comments, likes, and follows provided via YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok platform APIs.
  • Analytics & Metrics: Aggregated, non-personally identifiable viewer metrics (impressions, retention rates, general demographics) provided by platform analytics.

2. How We Use Information

Information collected is used solely to:

  • Deliver, optimize, and distribute educational video essays and shorts.
  • Respond to viewer comments, queries, and feedback.
  • Analyze content performance to create more informative educational videos.

3. Third-Party Integrations

Our distribution services utilize official APIs from Google (YouTube Data API), Meta (Instagram Graph API), and TikTok. Your use of those platforms is subject to their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Meta Data Policy: facebook.com/privacy/policy
  • TikTok Privacy Policy: tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy
